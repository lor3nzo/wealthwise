Method and source

Percentiles are interpolated on a log scale between published points: the cutoffs in Table A.2, plus each group's median treated as its midpoint. Above the 95th percentile the report gives no finer cutoffs, so the page shows 95+. Cutoffs use "usual" income while you enter last year's actual income. Treat ranks as approximate, within a few points.

Source: Changes in U.S. Family Finances from 2022 to 2025: Evidence from the Survey of Consumer Finances, Aladangady et al., Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, October 2026. All figures in 2025 dollars. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
